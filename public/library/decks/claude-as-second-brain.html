System Design

One brain. Two very different jobs.

Layer 1 — The General

Strategy & Thinking

Claude acts as your thinking partner. It has full context: your notes, journals, goals, calendar, and book highlights. You and Claude decide what to do — not how. Like a general who stays off the front line, this layer is about clarity, not execution.

Layer 2 — The Soldiers

Project Execution

Each project opens as its own Obsidian vault with its own Claude.md, skills, and context. Claude in this layer is a digital employee with one job: help you ship this specific project. It doesn't worry about everything else.

The secret is the separation. Strategy lives in Layer 1. Doing lives in Layer 2.

02

Bonus Principles

Four things most people get wrong about AI

From the video's closing section — the mindset layer that makes the system actually work.

The market only cares about outputs

Automating a process is pointless if the quality drops. If automation degrades the result, do that part yourself until you can automate it without the drop-off.

Use AI to get your time back

The real win isn't doing less — it's reclaiming hours so your human creativity and judgment can go where they matter most. KJ spent eight hours on a thumbnail because Claude did the research.

AI is a skill — go accumulate reps

Nobody has 10,000 hours in this yet. The field is wide open. Stop watching tutorials and go use it. The people winning at AI have simply been talking to it longer.

Have fun

Start with a project that excites you. Don't try to automate your whole life on day one. Build one fun thing, see what happens, and let the skill compound from there.
