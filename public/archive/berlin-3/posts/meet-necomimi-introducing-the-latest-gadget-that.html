Meet NecoMimi

Introducing the latest gadget that basically augments your thoughts through series of cute movements in form of rabbit ears. NecoMimi ears will move or wiggle based on the variety of activity you currently having when wearing the gadget. In a nutshell, it moves the ears by sensing your brain wave. The sensor, one press to your forehead and another attached to your left ear, will recognize at least four type of brain mode or activity: In relax mode, the sensor will bring both ears down. When you’re in focus or fully engaged will erect both ears toward any object/person you currently talking to. But just like any regular rabbit ears, it was curiosity that makes them wiggles.

Interestingly enough the sensor doesn’t always work on everyone. One of my classmate put it on and was immediately disappointed when nothing happen after several tries. Curiously enough, when the same person tried it again after certain amount of alcohol intake, the Necomimi starts to sense some kind of brain activity. How or why? Well, your guess is as good as mine! ;-)
