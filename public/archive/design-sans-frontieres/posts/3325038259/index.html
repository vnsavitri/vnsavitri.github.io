Zip-lining in Laos (part 1)

I recently did jungle trek in Bokeo Province in Northern Laos near the border of Thailand. Not only that one has to trek the seemingly endless path up high in the mountains, but some of the routes also required quite a lot of zip-lining; crossing numerous cliffs and jungle at such dizzying height and speed. Here’s some video that I was able to capture while hanging (sans helmet*) up in the air. This part of the trek was definitely not for the faint of heart!
