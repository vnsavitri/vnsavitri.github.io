Discover what it is...

The strong creature from before the Flood
Without flesh, without bone,
Without vein, without blood,
Without head, without feet…
In field, in forest…
Without hand, without foot.

It is also wide
As the surface of the earth,
And it was not born,
Nor was it seen…
