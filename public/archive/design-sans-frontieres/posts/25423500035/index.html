China Wrap Up

In the following weeks, provided that I’d have decent Internet connection, I’m hoping to jot down my travel trip on this blog. It’d be different from my usual design-related entries but hopefully still relevant as it falls under the ‘Wanderlust’ category.

This 'China Wrap Up’ trip would be some kind of homage to the country that has been my home for over seven years. I will be on the road with two friends, who coincidentally also on the verge of leaving* China. We are going to hike and trek around the southwestern part of China starting in Yunnan province and we’ll play the rest by ear. Hopefully I’d have enough time to end up as far south as Macau and taking the train back to Shanghai. We shall see. I was told that the best travel plan is to not have any. Simply go with the flow.

–
* My friend Simon just finished his school term and due to return to Montreal, his brother Nathaniel is off for a 2-year stint in Ghana. And I myself will embark on another adventure in Munich in July.
