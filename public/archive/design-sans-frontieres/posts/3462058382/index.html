Some (not so geeky) reading recommendations

Here’s a list of my favourite non-fiction readings that are a bit unrelated to the usual geeky stuff that I posted in this blog. Yes, I do read books without pictures! This was a short list that I sent out to a friend recently… Happy reading!

// Tiziano Terzani

- Behind the Forbidden Door (China)

- Giai Phong! The Fall & Liberation of Saigon

- Goodnight, Mister Lenin: A Journey Through the End of the Soviet Empire

- Letter Against the War (Afghanistan)

- A Fortune-Teller Told Me: Earthbound Travels in the Far East (This book inspired me to do my 5.5 months sabbatical on land across Southeast Asia many many moons ago!)

// Amin Maalouf
- The Crusades Through the Arab Eyes

- Leo the African (Travel in the 14th century, the fall of Granada to the Ottoman Empire, etc) -

- In the Name of Identity: Violence and the Need to Belong-

Try also “The Balthazar Odyssey”, “A Century After Beatrice”, “Samarkand” (About the brilliant Omar Khayyam and his famous Rubaayaat, etc). - I’ve read all his books and loved it.

// Karl Sabbagh - Palestine, A Personal History (offers different perspective from the usual ‘western’ point of view)


// Stephen Schwartz - The Other Islam: Sufism and the Road to Global Harmony


// Paul Kiwaczek - In Search of Zarathustra: Across Iran and Central Asia to Find the World’s First Prophet
