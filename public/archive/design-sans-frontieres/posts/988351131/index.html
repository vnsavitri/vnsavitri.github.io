Saturday Sailing ~ on Dianshan Lake, Shanghai.

Open water sailing is legally not allowed in China, so if you love sailing, this is probably not the right country to be in. However, some of us are willing to make do by sailing on the lake. I know it’s sad, but hey… beggar can’t be choosy! 

Considering its huge size, the Dianshan Lake [http://is.gd/euHAk] is not bad, really, especially if one merely wanted a quick getaway from the city.
