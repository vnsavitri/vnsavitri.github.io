Voluntary Data Diet

Since last Friday, I’ve decided to disconnect myself from anything online, not necessarily off the grid physically, but definitely no online presence whatsoever; no email checking, Twitter, FB, FourSquare, Instagram, not even Google, and certainly no photos upload. The temptation (read: withdrawal syndrome) is a lot harder when one is not physically stuck in the arse-behind of nowhere. When you know that the (sometimes) much more interesting online happenings is mere a tap away. 

So why the self-imposed “Data Diet”, you asked?

For one, I’d like to know how far I could limit myself as far as data consumption is concern, and secondly, how bad I’m going to miss being online. I’ve done Data Diet before but never at the scale where I totally cut myself off. I did Social Media Diet last X-Mas, which wasn’t too difficult since technically I was still ‘allowed’ to check my email or Googling something. Three days, unfortunately, is too short to measure the real extend of my Data Diet, but it’s a start. (FYI: I have to be back online since Monday is work day and therefore avoiding email would not be possible – where’s that fax machine when you need one?). 

Is it liberating? You bet it is. In the past three days, I’ve managed to read 6 books*, took the dog for a run at the pier, going to the gym, going for a walk, dinner with friends, and catching up on Portal 2 and Killzone 3 on my PS3. 

While this may be nothing, at the very least, my data diet was contributing to less noise online! Some food for thoughts, eh?
