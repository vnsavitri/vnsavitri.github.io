mind provoking talk on changing education paradigm

Mind provoking talk on Changing Education Paradigm by Sir Ken Robinson  and brilliantly animated for your viewing pleasure. 

Interesting highlight about how our current educational system is not only designed for different era but actually dumbing down the young generation, and not enough encouragement or room for ‘Divergent Thinking’, which is an essential capacity for creativity.
