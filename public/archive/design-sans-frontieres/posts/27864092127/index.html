Turning into a frog...

Today was my first day as a frog. Yes, at frog design we refer to ourselves as ‘frog’ and naturally our natural habitat is a pond. We frogs don’t have office. We have pond.

In more ways than one this was a big move on my part. Literally. Not only did I move nearly half a globe away from a country of superlatives that is China, but I also start a new career in a completely different industry. On top of that, I am going to Munich, the former capital of the Bavarian Kingdom where beer and sausages rules, a great plus for a vegetarian and non drinker like myself. This must be the universe playing its best cosmic joke on me.

Irony aside, I do love the first impression of Munich but I won’t go on a rampage excitement on this now as you can read my dedicated records about my life in Munich separately. For now allow me to indulge my narcissistic side by recalling how awesome my first day as a frog. Simply put, it was effing fantastic!

We had this thing called MMM or Monday Morning Meeting, which I think could also stand as Marvelous Monday Meeting since it was fused with healthy dose of seriousness and laughter. And as luck would have it I also had the honor and opportunity to briefly meet with Mark Rolston, who’s been a very inspiring Creative Leader at frog globally.

Just when I thought that my first day at the pond couldn’t get any better, the afternoon was sweetly interrupted by myriad serving of ice creams and freshly baked waffles. And to my horror apparently this is a daily thing. Drat, I better start turning my ‘moderation button’ on else I’ll be super hyperactive with that much sugar in my blood…
