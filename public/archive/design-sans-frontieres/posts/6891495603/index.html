A Trip Through Twitter's Lazyweb

The term lazyweb is commonly used to describe the act of outsourcing question to people online, particularly when the people asking are either too lazy to research themselves, or have the excuse for not knowing the ‘right’ keyword to search. Although some questions may warrant lengthy answer that usually beyond Google algorithm (for that, use Quora, duh!), the rest could probably just rely on Twitter. Thanks to Twitter, more people around the world are asking lazyweb what they don’t want to Google themselves. 

Well, we all get a little lazy sometimes. Luckily, we’ve got Twitter to help us out. See what a trip down the Lazyweb look like here.
