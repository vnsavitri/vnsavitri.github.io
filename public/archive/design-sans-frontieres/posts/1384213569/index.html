The Guggenheim Picks the 25 Most Creative Web Videos

Interesting pick of the top talents who creatively works using the medium of online video. The 25 top videos were projected on the outside of the museum in New York last night, and also streamed live on You Tube.

The videos will also be shown at the Guggenheim Museums in New York, Bilbao, Berlin, and Venice over the next few days. For complete list, go here.
