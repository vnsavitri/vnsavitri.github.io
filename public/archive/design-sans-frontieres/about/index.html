Hello ^_^

My name is Vivid N. Savitri. I am the person behind this blog: a Sufi, wanderer and recovered game addict with a fascination for geeky things.

This archive is devoted to design, games and wanderlust—plus the random observations that catch my fancy. Expect interactive design, gaming, mobile, UX, typography, photography and travel.

I have lived and worked across continents, travelled to more than 45 countries, and remain creatively fuelled by curiosity, stories and the unfamiliar.
