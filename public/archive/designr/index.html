A short introduction

About Vivid

Vivid is a designer and design leader who creates products with character, clarity and a human point of view. Her work spans digital product design, IoT, voice, AI, games, AR/VR and public services.

Across Australia, Europe, Asia and the United States, Vivid has led multidisciplinary teams and worked with global brands, government organisations and cultural institutions. Curiosity, practical experimentation and thoughtful collaboration remain the common thread.
