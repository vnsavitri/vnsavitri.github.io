Disneyland Mobile Site

21 April 2014

visit mobile site

overview

in collaboration with Disney, we were assigned to help the internal Disney dev team with additional design for the Disneyland Mobile Site, and to deliver a better user experience on touch devices across platform.

role + responsibilities

creative lead

  • responsible for the UX design and usability of the mobile site
  • led team of designers and developers to deliver scalable mobile site that runs on both iOS and Android

**client**
Disneyland Resort | **usa**

deliverables
AR companion app featuring 10 interactive ghosts and ghouls from the annals of history like the Headless Horseman, Bloody Mary and Flying Dutchman.

The app was developed for iOS (iPhone/iPad) & Android (smartphone/tablet)

process

A combined waterfall and agile-led project management with daily stand-up, backlog and unified communication tool using Pivotal Tracker project management tool

tools

HTML 5, Adobe creative suites, test flight and GitHub repository for ongoing prototype and development progress that allows easy collaboration between Disney and Trigger team

some snapshots of the Disneyland mobile site as feature on iOS
