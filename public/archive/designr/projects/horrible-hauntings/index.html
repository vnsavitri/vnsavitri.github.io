Horrible Hauntings Interactive Book

21 April 2014

download app on App Store | Google Play

objectives

to create interactive augmented reality experience for readers of Goosebottom's children's book, Horrible Hauntings.

role + responsibilities

product lead and creative director

  • responsible for overall UX design and usability of the app
  • led team of designers, developers and 3D modelers/animators
  • facilitate the ideation session with Goosebottom Books

**client**
Goosebottom Books | **usa**

deliverables
AR companion app featuring 10 interactive ghosts and ghouls from the annals of history like the Headless Horseman, Bloody Mary and Flying Dutchman.

The app was developed for iOS (iPhone/iPad) & Android (smartphone/tablet)

process

Agile-led project management with daily stand-up, backlog and unified communication tool using ActiveCollab project management tool.

tools

unity 3D, autodesk maya, adobe creative suites, test flight and GitHub repository for ongoing prototype and development progress

deliverables snapsots
some random snapshots of the Horrible Hauntings companion app as featured on iPad
